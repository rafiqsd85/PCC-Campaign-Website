My Vision

A Stronger Club. A Better Community.

I believe a computer club should be more than a name on a notice board. It should be a place where every member can learn, compete, lead and grow.

As Vice President, I will work to make PCIU Computer Club active, organized and open to every member, with clear responsibilities and fair opportunities for all.

The Goal

Learn Together.
Compete Together.
Grow Together.

Every member deserves an opportunity to participate, contribute and be recognized.

My Commitments

Six priorities to build a more active, organized and inclusive PCIU Computer Club.

01

Working with the President

I will work closely with the President, share ideas openly and support decisions that benefit the club. I will handle disagreements respectfully and keep the club's interests first.

02

Tech Competitions

I will work toward regular technical events that help members develop skills, test ideas and showcase their creativity.

  • ✓ Programming and problem-solving contests
  • ✓ Web and app development challenges
  • ✓ Quizzes and tech talks
  • ✓ Project showcases
03

E-Sports Competitions

I will work to arrange well-managed e-sports tournaments with fair rules, proper scheduling and transparent results to build teamwork and community spirit.

04

Sports Competitions

I will encourage indoor and outdoor sports events that strengthen friendships, promote well-being and bring members together beyond technology.

05

Leadership & Responsibility

When the President is unavailable, I will take responsibility within my authority, make timely and fair decisions, keep club activities running and report back to the President.

06

Fair Committee Work

I will promote clear duties, fair task distribution and accountability across committees.

  • ✓ Assign tasks fairly
  • ✓ Set duties and deadlines
  • ✓ Follow up on progress
  • ✓ Recognize active contributors

My Promise to You

Leadership Through Action

I will be honest, active and approachable. I will listen to every member's suggestions, share responsibility rather than take credit and work to make PCIU Computer Club stronger than before.
